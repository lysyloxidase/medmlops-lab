# Disclaimer

MedMLOps-Lab is a portfolio demo and engineering reference. It is **not** a
medical device, clinical decision support product, diagnostic system, or a
replacement for professional medical judgment.

Models, metrics, calibration plots, conformal intervals, monitoring dashboards,
and fairness analyses produced by this project are educational artifacts. They
must not be used to make patient-care decisions without independent clinical,
regulatory, privacy, security, and institutional review.
